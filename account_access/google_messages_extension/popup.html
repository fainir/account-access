Use your signed-in account

In Account Access, choose “Use signed-in Chrome” for GitHub. Then open your signed-in GitHub tab and connect below.

GitHub session cookies pass directly to your encrypted local store. The website and username are checked before saving. Site and cookie access are removed after this transfer.

Connect your login SMS

Use your already-paired Google Messages tab. Your AI never receives login codes.

GitHub changes sender numbers. During its pending sign-in only, match a newly arriving text with the exact GitHub login-message template.

No messages are sent, deleted or marked as read. Codes pass to Account Access on this computer, then expire with the sign-in.
