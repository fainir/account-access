## Owner actions

1. After a successor package passes every Installer and release gate, install that exact package from its verified persistent path and approve the native macOS prompt -> unblocks F1 and the installed-service/agent checks for F6 and F8.
2. After F1 install, open `/Applications/Account Access.app`, choose **Upgrade AI connection**, and approve the separate **Authorize Account Access setup** dialog if it appears -> unblocks live F6 acceptance. Previous Mac approvals did not complete enrollment. Keep any password in the macOS dialog.
3. In Account Access > Verification, connect the approved Gmail account in Chrome and select **Check Google connection**. Then use a supported account with a real email-code challenge and complete it in Account Access -> unblocks private mailbox and delivery acceptance. Keep mailbox passwords and codes out of chat.
4. Open Account Access > Verification > Pair Android phone, scan its pairing QR and approve the requested access on Android -> unblocks real SMS acceptance. No phone code belongs in chat.
5. Sign in to Lemon Squeezy through Account Access once with your authenticator app -> confirms its private TOTP challenge and enrollment against the real site. Keep the password and codes in their intended apps.
6. Before any public listing, review [the release copy draft](docs/release-copy-draft.md) with qualified counsel, supply the privacy/support contact and mailing address where required, and confirm the final build's data/permission disclosures -> unblocks the legal/store gate.
