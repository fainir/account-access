# Complete release documents - 31 October 2026

Prepared complete [privacy notice](../../docs/privacy.md) and
[terms of use](../../docs/terms.md), replacing the previous summary-only handoff.
Both are explicitly release-approval drafts; neither is falsely marked published
or legally approved. Publisher name, business status and contact exactly match
the owner's supplied details.

The privacy notice incorporates the checked
[technical data disclosure](../../docs/data-handling.md) and adds voluntary
provision/consequences, access/correction and support-mail handling. It makes no
remote access/erase, all-fields encryption, automatic history deletion or
general website/MFA acceptance promise. It describes broad underlying Gmail and
Android permissions as well as the narrower application filtering. Existing
technical disclosure evidence and its 129 targeted tests remain valid: the Mac
application and Android runtime sources were not changed for these documents.

The terms cover publisher/contact, MIT and third-party licensing, authorized
operations, device and verification limits, independent providers/AI recipients,
data controls/recovery limits, updates/availability, the absence of an included
subscription/cloud service, license warranty/liability wording with mandatory
rights preserved, support and ending use. They impose no new payment, personal
guarantee, mandatory governing-law forum or withdrawal of existing MIT rights.

Research used the [Privacy Protection Authority's current duties guidance](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/manager_duties/he/InfoDuties_new.pdf)
for the notice topics: voluntary provision, purpose, identity/contact, recipients
and access/correction. The [official MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit)
was compared with the existing repository license. Preparing these topics is not
a certification that every jurisdiction's legal requirements have been met.

Local Markdown links and the exact public contact were checked, and no empty
publisher/contact placeholders remain. Final publisher/legal approval remains
the owner gate. No store submission, legal acceptance or public release occurred.
